Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Seovaro ("we", "us", "our") operates the seovaro.online website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our site or engage our enterprise SEO and growth strategy services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, email address, and company name, when you reach out via our contact form, WhatsApp, or email.
  • Usage data collected automatically, including pages visited, browser type, device information, and general location, through analytics tools such as Google Analytics.
  • Newsletter subscription information, limited to the email address you submit.
  • Communications you send us, including strategy briefs, engagement details, and correspondence related to our services.

How We Use Information

Information we collect is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested enterprise SEO and growth strategy services.
  • Send newsletters and strategic intelligence to subscribers who opt in.
  • Improve our website, content, and service offerings based on usage patterns.
  • Meet legal, contractual, and regulatory obligations.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who assist in operating our website and delivering services (for example, hosting, analytics, and email delivery providers), and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those functions on our behalf.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies, including Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use our site and to improve the browsing experience. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not function properly without them.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect the information we hold. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
