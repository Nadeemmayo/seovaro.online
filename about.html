Our story

Why Seovaro exists

Seovaro was founded by Nadeem Mayo after years delivering SEO and link building behind the scenes for agencies across North America and the UK.

He saw established companies receiving the same templated SEO as brand-new startups, with reports that rarely connected to revenue. Seovaro was created to offer something calmer and more considered: senior strategy, careful execution and honest measurement.

Today we focus on established Canadian businesses, helping them protect and grow search revenue and put AI to sensible use.
